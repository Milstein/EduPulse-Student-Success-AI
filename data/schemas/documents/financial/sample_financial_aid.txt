# Financial Aid Guide

## FAFSA Application

### Important Deadlines
- Federal Deadline: June 30
- State Deadline: March 1 (varies by state)
- Institutional Deadline: February 15

### Required Documents
- Social Security Number
- Federal tax returns (previous year)
- W-2 forms and records of income
- Bank statements and investment records
- Records of untaxed income

### How to Apply
1. Create an FSA ID at studentaid.gov
2. Complete the FAFSA form online
3. List our school (Federal School Code: 001234)
4. Submit and sign electronically
5. Review your Student Aid Report (SAR)

## Types of Financial Aid

### Grants (Gift Aid - No Repayment Required)
- **Pell Grant**: Up to $7,395/year (based on need)
- **FSEOG**: $100-$4,000/year (for exceptional need)
- **State Grant**: Varies by state and eligibility

### Scholarships
- **Merit Scholarship**: $1,000-$10,000/year (based on GPA/test scores)
- **Need-Based Scholarship**: $500-$5,000/year (based on FAFSA)
- **Departmental Scholarship**: Varies by department
- **External Scholarships**: Various amounts from private organizations

### Work-Study
- Part-time employment opportunities
- Up to 15 hours/week during academic year
- Jobs available on and off campus
- Hourly rate: $12-$15/hour

### Student Loans
- **Direct Subsidized Loans**: Up to $5,500/year (need-based, government pays interest while in school)
- **Direct Unsubsidized Loans**: Up to $20,500/year (interest accrues immediately)
- **Perkins Loans**: Up to $5,500/year (for exceptional need)

## Eligibility Requirements

### General Requirements
- U.S. citizen or eligible noncitizen
- Enrolled at least half-time (6 credits)
- Maintaining satisfactory academic progress (SAP)
- Not in default on federal student loans
- Selective Service registration (if applicable)

### Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP)
- Minimum GPA: 2.0
- Completion Rate: 67% of attempted credits
- Maximum Timeframe: 150% of program length

## Tuition Payment Plans

### Plan Options
- **Full Payment**: Due by registration deadline
- **Two-Payment Plan**: 50% at registration, 50% mid-semester
- **Monthly Plan**: 4-5 equal monthly installments

### Fees
- One-time enrollment fee: $50
- Late payment fee: $25
- Returned payment fee: $30

## Financial Aid Appeal Process

### When to Appeal
- Special circumstances not reflected on FAFSA
- Loss of employment
- Medical expenses
- Family changes (divorce, death)

### How to Appeal
1. Complete the Financial Aid Appeal Form
2. Write a detailed letter explaining circumstances
3. Provide supporting documentation
4. Submit to Financial Aid Office
5. Allow 2-3 weeks for review
