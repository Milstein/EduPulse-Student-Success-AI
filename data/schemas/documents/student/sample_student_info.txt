# Sample Student Knowledge Base Document

## Registration Deadlines

### Fall Semester
- Early Registration: April 1 - May 15
- Regular Registration: August 1 - August 20
- Late Registration: August 21 - August 27 (with fee)

### Spring Semester
- Early Registration: November 1 - December 15
- Regular Registration: January 5 - January 15
- Late Registration: January 16 - January 20 (with fee)

## Academic Policies

### Grading Scale
- A: 90-100%
- B: 80-89%
- C: 70-79%
- D: 60-69%
- F: Below 60%

### Academic Probation
Students are placed on academic probation when their GPA falls below 2.0.

### Dean's List
Students with a GPA of 3.5 or higher are recognized on the Dean's List each semester.

### Library
